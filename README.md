# Optimize Evidence

A multi-agent AI research platform that continuously reviews, critiques, and synthesizes emerging evidence in regenerative medicine, sports science, recovery, performance, and well-being.

## Mission

Optimize Evidence helps healthcare practitioners understand what new research means, how strong the evidence is, and whether it is relevant to their patients.

The platform is designed to support professional judgment—not replace clinical evaluation or provide autonomous medical advice.

## Version 1

The first version will:

1. Retrieve recent research from PubMed.
2. Save each paper with its title, abstract, authors, journal, date, and source.
3. Ask specialized AI researchers to review the evidence.
4. Produce a concise practitioner briefing.
5. Display the results in a calm, readable dashboard.

## AI Research Team

### Research Librarian
Finds relevant studies, removes duplicates, and organizes the evidence.

### Regenerative Medicine Specialist
Explains biological mechanisms and distinguishes plausible mechanisms from demonstrated human outcomes.

### Clinical Evidence Reviewer
Evaluates study design, patient population, outcomes, safety, limitations, and clinical applicability.

### Sports Performance Scientist
Examines implications for performance, recovery, rehabilitation, and return to sport.

### Research Director
Combines the analyses, identifies agreement and disagreement, and produces the final evidence briefing.

## Initial Research Areas

- PRP and orthobiologics
- Tendon and ligament recovery
- Cartilage and joint health
- Muscle injury and regeneration
- Bone health
- Athletic recovery and performance longevity

## Evidence Principles

- Every research claim must link to its source.
- Human and preclinical evidence must be clearly separated.
- Clinical relevance and evidence confidence are different measures.
- AI-generated interpretations must be labeled as interpretations.
- Preliminary findings must not be presented as established clinical facts.
